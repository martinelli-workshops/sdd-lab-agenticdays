# Glossary

| Term               | Definition                                                                                                                                              | Avoid                          |
|--------------------|---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------|--------------------------------|
| Guest              | A person who reserves a room or stays at the hotel. A guest has no account and is identified by name and email address.                                 | Customer, Client               |
| Staff Member       | An employee who signs in to the system with one role: Receptionist, Housekeeper, or Manager.                                                            | User, Employee                 |
| Receptionist       | A staff member at the front desk who handles reservations, check-in, check-out, and payment on site.                                                    | Clerk, Front Desk              |
| Housekeeper        | A staff member who cleans rooms and reports their room status.                                                                                          | Housekeeping, Cleaner, Maid    |
| Manager            | A staff member who maintains room categories, rooms, and staff members, views the reports, and can perform all functions of the other roles.     | Owner, Admin, Administrator    |
| Reservation        | A guest's claim on one room category for a date range from arrival date to departure date. A room is assigned only at check-in.                         | Booking                        |
| Reservation Number | The unique number the system gives a reservation; together with the guest's email address it lets the guest view or cancel it.                          | Booking Number, Booking Code   |
| Arrival Date       | The first night of a reservation; the day the guest is expected to check in.                                                                            | Start Date, Check-in Date      |
| Departure Date     | The day the guest leaves; the night before it is the last night of the reservation.                                                                     | End Date, Check-out Date       |
| Room Category      | A kind of room with a name, a maximum number of persons, and a nightly price, for example Single or Double. Guests reserve a room category, not a room. | Room Type                      |
| Room               | One of the hotel's physical rooms, identified by its room number and belonging to exactly one room category.                                            |                                |
| Nightly Price      | The price of one night in a room category. The total price of a reservation is the nightly price times the number of nights.                            | Rate, Tariff                   |
| Availability       | The number of rooms of a room category that are neither reserved nor out of service on every night of a date range.                                     | Vacancy                        |
| Check-in           | The event at which the receptionist assigns a clean room to the guest of a reservation; the room becomes occupied.                                      |                                |
| Check-out          | The event at which the guest leaves, the total price is shown, and the room status becomes Dirty.                                                       |                                |
| Cancellation       | Ending a reservation before check-in, by the guest or by a receptionist; the room category becomes available again.                                     | Storno                         |
| No-Show            | A reservation whose guest did not check in by the end of the arrival date.                                                                              |                                |
| Guest Registration | The personal data (address, nationality, date of birth, identity document) the hotel must record for each guest at check-in.                            | Registration Form, Meldeschein |
| Room Status        | The cleaning state of a room: Clean, Dirty, or Out of Service. Only a Clean room can be assigned at check-in.                                           | Cleaning Status                |
| Out of Service     | A room status that removes a room from availability for a date range, for example during a repair.                                                      | Blocked, Maintenance           |
| Room Defect        | A problem in a room that a housekeeper reports to the manager, such as a broken lamp.                                                                   | Damage, Issue                  |
| Payment            | The record that a guest paid the total price of a stay at check-out. The system does not process the payment itself.                                    |                                |
| Occupancy Rate     | The number of occupied room nights divided by the number of room nights not out of service in a period, as a percentage.                                | Utilization                    |
| Revenue            | The sum of recorded payments in a period.                                                                                                               | Turnover, Income               |
